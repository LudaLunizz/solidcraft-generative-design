Generative Design Engine: user manual

SolidCraft plugin, version 0.6.1 (tested on SolidCraft 0.45.0). Written 21 Nov 2026. This manual covers day-to-day use. The README in the repository has the measurements, the sources and the reasons behind the design.
Repository: github.com/LudaLunizz/Generative-design-plugin-for-SolidCraft
In short: the plugin adds six commands to SolidCraft. With them you put real engineering materials on your bodies, read mass and strength per body, check how much stress a flat part gets under a load, and let the computer design a light plate for you. Next to the plugin there are five command-line tools that do the heavy work (3D parts, finer plates, beams) outside SolidCraft's time limits.

Two things to know first

  • Third-party plugin. It is made by LudaLunizz, not by Tangency Labs or the SolidCraft team, and has no official connection to them. Questions and bugs go to this plugin's repository.
  • A design aid, not a certified analysis. The numbers can be wrong. A qualified person must check them before anyone relies on them for a design, manufacturing or safety decision.

1. What it does

CommandKindWhat you get
Materials (materials)readThe library: every material with stiffness, density and strength, and the allowable stress at your safety factor.
Apply material (apply-material)editPuts a library material with its full engineering card in the design and assigns it to one body or to all. One undo step.
Material report (material-report)readPer body: material, volume, mass, stiffness, yield and tensile strength, allowable stress.
Stress check (stress-check)readFor a flat part: where it is held, where the force pushes, how hard. Gives peak stress and where, safety factor (pass/fail), largest displacement, a text heat map.
Optimise plate (optimise-plate)editDesigns a light, stiff plate (a cantilever or a beam) with a material share you choose, then draws it as a sketch and extrudes it into a new body with the material on it. One undo step.
Solver benchmark (solver-benchmark)readA development tool: runs the optimiser on a test case and reports the work done. You do not need it for design work.

"Read" commands never change the design. "Edit" commands change it as one step, so one Undo removes all of it (sketch, body and material together).

2. Install, update, check

Install or update

  1. Download gen-design-engine-<version>.zip and its .sha256 from the latest GitHub release.
  2. Check the hash: sha256sum gen-design-engine-*.zip and compare it with the number in the .sha256 file. (The v0.5.0 file once named the path dist/...; comparing by eye always works.)
  3. In SolidCraft: Preferences › Plugins › Install plugin… (File › Plugins… opens the same page) and pick the zip. Installing a newer version over an older one replaces it.
  4. No restart is needed: SolidCraft 0.45.0 picks up the new version in a window that is already open (the buttons change right away). If the commands or labels do not change, restart SolidCraft.

The plugin asks for no folders, no network, no clock and no extra permissions. It stays inside SolidCraft's default limits, so SolidCraft asks for no extra approval.

Check what is installed

jq -r .version ~/.local/share/solidcraft/plugins/gen-design-engine/plugin.json
solidcraft-cli plugin check ~/.local/share/solidcraft/plugins/gen-design-engine

On Linux the plugin folder is ~/.local/share/solidcraft/plugins/gen-design-engine (also since SolidCraft 0.44, where the app ID changed).

3. Where to find it in SolidCraft

Checked in SolidCraft 0.45.0. All six commands are buttons in the Plugins group at the right end of the Model tab, each labelled with its command name: apply-material, material-report, materials, stress-check, optimise-plate, solver-benchmark. Hover over a button for "Generative Design Engine · name". Clicking the word Plugins under the group opens the Plugins menu with the same list.

Version 0.6.0 and older asked for places on the Manage and Inspect tabs; SolidCraft then labelled the Model-tab buttons "Manage" and "Inspect" instead of their names. Since 0.6.1 the plugin asks for no tab, so the buttons show their names and the commands appear only on the Model tab.

  • Each command opens a form drawn from the plugin's field list. Most fields have a default; some have a short help text (hover over it). Fixed faces and Load faces in the stress check have no default and must be filled in.
  • Body fields are plain text: type the body name as the browser shows it. Empty means every body (or, for the stress check, the only body).
  • Opening a form runs nothing; only Run does.

4. Materials: list, apply, report

Materials

Pick a family (all, metals, plastics) and a safety factor. You get the table of section 10 with the allowable stress worked out. It runs no app commands, so it is safe at any time.

Apply material

FieldMeaning
MaterialOne of the 14 library materials.
BodyOne body name, or empty for every body.

The material is created in the design once, with every value naming its source. SolidCraft marks those values "source review required": that is normal. If your design already has a material with the same name but other values, the plugin refuses instead of overwriting it, and says so. Rename your own material or pick another.

Material report

Pick a body (or empty for all) and a safety factor (1 to 10, default 2). For each body you get material, volume, mass, stiffness and strengths, and the allowable stress = yield strength ÷ safety factor (for printed plastics, which have no yield value: tensile strength ÷ safety factor).

The report is not a stress analysis. It says how much stress the material may take, not how much your part gets. For that, use the stress check.

If a body's material has a library name but another density, the report leaves out the strengths (it cannot be sure they belong to that material).

5. Stress check

For a flat part of one thickness (a plate, a bracket cut from sheet, a printed flat part). It cuts the part into a grid of small cells, holds it where you say, pushes where you say, and works out the stress in every cell.

The form

FieldMeaning
Fixed facesWhere the part is held. See "naming faces" below. Several with commas.
Load facesWhere the force acts. The force is spread evenly over them.
Force X / Y / ZThe force in newtons (default 100 N down along −Y). 1 kg weighs about 9.81 N.
BodyThe body to check; empty when the design has only one.
Material"The body's own material" (default) or any library material, to try another without changing the body.
Safety factor neededThe check passes when the real safety factor is at least this (default 2).
Plate planeAutomatic: the plane across the thinnest direction. Or choose XY, XZ, YZ.
Cells along the longer side10 to 400, default 240. More is finer and slower.
Text heat mapA picture of the stress made of characters, in the reply.

Naming faces

  • A side of the part: -x, +x, -y, +y, -z, +z. Easiest for a plain plate: -x is the left end.
  • A face name as SolidCraft's model.faces lists it, for example F1:-x or F2:bore:side:0 (the inside of a hole).
  • A face number.
  • Supports can be holes (the cylinder face through the thickness, i.e. a bolt) or flat side faces.

Reading the result

  • Peak von Mises stress (MPa) and where it is. Von Mises is the usual "how hard is the material working" number for metals and plastics.
  • Safety factor = strength ÷ peak stress, and pass/fail against the factor you asked for.
  • Largest displacement (mm): how far the part bends.
  • 99th percentile stress: sharp inside corners give a peak that keeps rising with finer grids. If the plugin warns about that, judge by the 99th percentile or round the corner in the model.
  • Warnings: read them. The part of the force that points through the plate is ignored (bending out of the plate is not covered), and a part that is thick or changes through its thickness gets a warning.

Checked against beam theory: a 100 × 10 × 5 mm aluminium cantilever under 100 N gives 1.170 mm tip deflection (theory 1.17) and 121 MPa peak (theory 120).

6. Plate optimiser

You give a rectangle, a material, a load and how much of the rectangle may be material. The plugin removes what is not needed for stiffness and draws what is left as smooth outlines, then extrudes it into a new body.

The form

FieldDefaultMeaning
Width200 mmAlong X. The fixed edge is at X = 0.
Height100 mmAlong Y. Rounded to whole grid cells; the reply gives the height actually used.
Thickness10 mmExtrude depth.
Material6061-T6Library material put on the new body.
Load casecantileverCantilever: left edge fixed, load at the right middle (a shelf bracket). MBB half beam: half of a beam on two supports, load at the top left (the other half is its mirror).
Force500 NOnly used for the deflection estimate. The shape does not depend on it.
Material share0.40.1 to 0.9: how much of the rectangle stays. Lower = lighter but bendier.
Cells across the width8020 to 240. More = finer detail, longer run. Rounded to a multiple of 4.
Design iterations6010 to 150. More = a cleaner design.
Smallest member2 cells1 to 4. Larger gives fewer, thicker members.
Corner rounding0 mmFillet radius where members meet. Only adds material (a few per cent), never thins a member. Holes narrower than the fillet close.
Body nameOptimised plateMust not exist yet in the design.

Size limits (SolidCraft gives a plugin 30 s)

  • The whole grid is at most 30,000 cells, and cells × iterations at most 2.4 million.
  • Examples that fit: 160 × 80 cells with 150 iterations (about 3 s); 240 × 120 cells with about 80 iterations (about 5 s).
  • A plate that is too thin (under 4 cells high) or too tall and narrow is refused with a message saying what to change.
  • For finer or rounder plates, use the refine pass plate_refine (section 9). The reply points to it.

What you get

A sketch of closed smooth outlines (splines; the plate's own edges stay straight), an extrude, and the material on the body. The reply gives mass, number of outlines and holes, and a rough deflection at the load point.

Stiffness only. The optimiser does not check strength, stress concentrations, buckling or whether it can be made. Always run the stress check on the new body afterwards.

7. A full example: from plate to checked part

  1. Optimise plate: width 200, height 100, thickness 10, material petg-printed, cantilever, force 50 N, share 0.4, cells 160, iterations 150, rounding 3 mm, name Bracket.
  2. Look at it. Too spindly? Undo once and raise the share or the smallest member.
  3. Stress check: body Bracket, fixed faces -x, load faces +x, force Y −50, safety factor 2.
  4. Fails? Raise the share, the thickness or the rounding (sharp corners carry the peaks) and run both again.
  5. Material report: mass for the slicer or the order.
  6. Save the design (.sc) and export the STL for printing.

8. Using it from Claude, MCP or a script

Since SolidCraft 0.45 the installed plugin's commands are also MCP tools. Claude sees them as gen-design-engine_materials, _apply-material, _material-report, _stress-check, _optimise-plate, _solver-benchmark on the solidcraft server. You can just ask: "put PETG on the bracket and stress-check it with 50 N down at +x".

In a script or batch, the command is plugin.run:

{"command": "plugin.run",
 "params": {"path": "gen-design-engine", "entry": "stress-check",
            "params": {"fixed": "-x", "load": "+x", "force_y": -50, "body": "Bracket"}}}

The reply is wrapped as {"result": ...}. Scripts may pass "bodies": ["A", "B"] instead of "body" (not both). A headless run (solidcraft-cli run) first needs plugin.inspect for the approval hash; tests/cli-test.sh shows how.

9. Command-line tools

These run outside SolidCraft, natively, with no time or memory limit. They are experimental. Build them once in the repository folder:

cd Generative-design-plugin-for-SolidCraft
cargo build --release --features gpu --bins     # without --features gpu: CPU only

The programs land in target/release/. Each file in src/bin/ starts with its full usage. Long runs can be kept to some of the cores with taskset (for example taskset -c 0-9).

ToolUse it for
plate_refineThe plate optimiser, finer (up to 800 cells) and rounder, on the GPU. Writes a script that builds the body in SolidCraft.
stress3d3D stress check of any STL part against a library material. Heat-map PNGs + report.json.
stress3d_printedThe same for a 3D-printed part: walls and skins around a lighter infill, layers along Z.
eso3d"Start with a pyramid and take out what is not needed": a wall bracket grown from a solid frustum, with optional countersunk screw holes. check=FILE.stl checks any STL with the same loads.
beam_optLightest plate-truss for a long cantilever beam, with buckling checks.

plate_refine

target/release/plate_refine width=200 height=100 thickness=10 material=petg-printed \
  share=0.4 cells=480 min_member=3 round=4 name="Refined plate" --out refine.json
solidcraft-cli run refine.json        # or ask Claude: MCP batch {"path": ...}

It stops by itself when the design has settled (limit 3,000 iterations; a 480 × 240 run took about 3 minutes). min_member and round are in mm. The GPU is capped at 80 % busy, desktop included (gpu_max, default 80; do not raise it). Without a suitable GPU it uses the CPU.

stress3d

target/release/stress3d part.stl --material aluminium-6061-t6 \
  --fix zmin --pin z,50,30,4 --case pull:0,0,-200 --voxel 1.5 --sf 2 --out out/
  • --fix SIDE: the face that is held (xmin … zmax).
  • --pin A,C1,C2,R: the load goes in through a bore along axis A at the other two coordinates, radius R.
  • --case NAME:FX,FY,FZ: a load case in N (several allowed; an optional :MX,MY,MZ twist in N·mm).
  • --voxel: cube size in mm. Smaller is finer and slower (1.5 mm on a 150 mm foot: about 40 s).

stress3d_printed

As stress3d, plus the print settings: --walls 8 --top-layers 8 --bottom-layers 3 --layer 0.2 --infill 0.25. --infill-exp 1 gives the stiff (upper) bound, 2 the soft (lower) bound: run both if unsure. The part stands on its zmin face as printed.

eso3d

taskset -c 0-9 target/release/eso3d reach=150 base_y=80 base_z=100 tip=20 voxel=2.5 \
  force=50 side=15 material=petg-printed sf=4 holes=20:30,-20:30 out=run1

Writes the voxel STL and a smooth STL. Holes default to Ø4.5 with a Ø8.4, 90° countersink (hole=, sink=).

Loads: by default two cases, force down alone and side sideways alone (one way). loads=real puts them together and checks the side load both ways; knock=1.5 (1 to 10) multiplies every force as a margin for knocks. Set force to what really hangs on the bracket (N = kg × 9.81).

Printed parts: print=x, y or z tells it which way the layers stack (x: wall plate on the bed, y: on its side, z: upright). A print is weaker across its layers (PETG 30 MPa against 47 along them), so every spot is judged by whichever is worse: the normal stress, or what pulls the layers apart, scaled up by 47/30. The strength across the layers is known for the printed library plastics; for others give it with z_mpa=. The bars report also lists all three orientations from the same run: the safety factor for each and the bars that would need support (flatter than 45°).

beam_opt

target/release/beam_opt length=5000 depth=400 elem=10 load=98.1 gpu=1 jobs=10 --out beam/

A starting point only: for long beams the weight is set by buckling, and a tube often wins (it did for the 5 m beam).

For printed parts: the library holds the strength along the layers. Across the layers (Z) a print is much weaker: PLA 38, PETG 30, ABS 32, ASA 31, PAHT-CF 47, PA12 42 MPa. If your part is pulled apart across its layers, use a higher safety factor or turn the part on the print bed. Each plastic is one brand; other brands differ. PAHT-CF that has taken up moisture is weaker than its card.

To add or change a material: edit materials/library.json, run ./build.sh (it regenerates plugin.json, runs every check and packs the zip), then tests/cli-test.sh, then install the new zip.

11. Limits

  • Linear elastic, small deflections, rigid supports, evenly spread loads. No buckling, fatigue, creep, heat or contact (except the experimental contact in eso3d).
  • The stress check in SolidCraft is 2D: flat parts of one thickness, loads in the plane of the plate. For real 3D parts use stress3d on an exported STL.
  • The plate optimiser designs for stiffness under one load; it knows nothing of strength or how the part is made.
  • SolidCraft refuses to hand over very large meshes (over about 8,192 points), so big imported parts cannot be checked inside the plugin. Export the STL and use stress3d.
  • No GPU inside SolidCraft: the plugin interface has no compute access. Only the command-line tools use the GPU.

12. Troubleshooting

Message or problemWhat to do
Commands missing or old after an installRestart SolidCraft (normally not needed). Then check the version (section 2).
Run button greyed outA field the dialog cannot draw: use the body text field, not a list.
"the design has several bodies; name one"Fill in the Body field.
"… is not closed, so it has no inside to analyse"The body is a surface or has a gap. Fix the model, then check again.
"material is named … but has other values than the library's"Your own material shares a library name. Pick a library material in the form, or rename yours.
"name a face (e.g. F1:-x), its number, or a side such as -x"The face text was not understood. Use a side (-x) or copy the name from model.faces.
"too thin for the grid" / "too tall and narrow"Raise the cells, or change the width-to-height ratio.
"the design already has a body named …"Pick another Body name or delete the old body.
"the optimiser left no material at the fixed edge"Raise the material share or the iterations.
Out of fuel / takes too longFewer cells or iterations, or use plate_refine.
Peak stress rises every time you refine the gridA sharp inside corner. Round it (Corner rounding, or a fillet) or judge by the 99th percentile.
Warning: force through the plate ignoredOut-of-plane bending is not covered; use stress3d.
